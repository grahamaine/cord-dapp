Suppliers on other blockchains verify an Ethereum, Base or Solana agent through its link to Concordium

A supplier agent on another chain can link its key to a Concordium agent or owner (CIS-8). Kakasa looks the key up, finds the identity-verified owner, and runs the same badge check. Verified suppliers can be paid on their own chain in EURC (Base Sepolia, Solana devnet), or on Concordium.

EVM addresses don't reveal their key: paste the public key, or let the supplier sign once with MetaMask.
