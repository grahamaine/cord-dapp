    The problem

    Paying many suppliers is where money leaks

    Organisations in Uganda pay many service providers, large and small. The same three problems keep coming up, and they get worse once AI agents start making payments, because an agent can't tell who is really behind a payee.

    ?

    Ghost or duplicate suppliers

    Money sent to a payee nobody can identify, or to the same supplier twice.

    !

    Unauthorised payments

    Amounts above what was approved, or spending that runs past the budget.

    §

    Gaps at audit time

    Payments with no clear record of who approved them, who received them, and why.

    How it works

    Five checks on every payment

    If any rule fails, the agent refuses before a signature exists and says why.

    1

    Buyer verified

    The supplier agent only serves buyers with a Verified by Concordium badge.

    2

    Payee verified

    The buyer checks the payee owns an active, badged agent tied to a real identity.

    3

    Owner's limits

    Per-payment cap, daily limit, allowed assets and an approved-supplier list.

    4

    Sponsored payment

    Paid over x402 on Concordium. Cord's facilitator covers the network fee.

    5

    Receipt trail

    Both sides add a hash-chained receipt linking the payment to two verified owners.

    Built on Concordium

    Under the hood

    Agent Registry · CIS-8004

    Each agent is an on-chain record pointing to its Agent Card, pinned by a SHA-256 hash.

    Verified by Concordium

    Ties every agent to an identity-verified owner account. Checked by both sides.

    x402 payments

    The supplier answers "402 Payment Required"; the buyer pays and retries in one flow.

    Sponsored transactions

    Cord's facilitator pays network fees, so small invoices stay cheap.

    Stablecoins · PLT

    Invoices priced in a stablecoin such as USDR at full value. On testnet, small CCD amounts.

    MCP

    An owner can instruct the purchasing agent through Claude or any MCP client.
